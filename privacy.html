POLICY.

This page explains what information MonkeRoom may process through the game, website and Discord bot, why it may be used, and how we approach privacy.

PRIMAL THEORY · MONKEROOM · LEGAL DOCUMENT
Last Updated · November 23, 2026

01Information We Collect

MonkeRoom is designed to collect as little personal information as reasonably necessary to provide its services.

Depending on how you interact with MonkeRoom, certain technical, gameplay, Discord, or moderation information may be processed.

  • IP address and connection information
  • Browser and device information
  • Player, account, or Discord identifiers
  • In-game username
  • Multiplayer session information
  • Game statistics
  • Discord server configuration and command usage
  • Moderation, anti-cheat, and diagnostic information

02How We Use Information

Information may be used to operate, secure, maintain, and improve MonkeRoom, its website, Discord bot, and community.

  • Provide and maintain game and bot functionality
  • Display game status and basic statistics
  • Operate multiplayer functionality
  • Prevent cheating, abuse, spam, and malicious activity
  • Moderate the community
  • Diagnose technical problems
  • Improve the game, bot, and website
  • Maintain service security and stability

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

03Discord Bot

If you use or install the MonkeRoom Discord bot, the bot may process information required for its features, such as Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel or role configuration, command usage, and moderation or diagnostic data.

The bot does not need access to private Discord messages unless a specific feature explicitly requires such access and the necessary permissions are granted.

04Game Information

When playing MonkeRoom, information necessary for multiplayer functionality, moderation, security, and gameplay may be processed.

The website or Discord bot may display non-sensitive game information such as current player count, active rooms, game version, or general service status.

We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information through the game.

05Discord

MonkeRoom provides a Discord community for players. If you choose to join or interact with the Discord server, your use of Discord is subject to Discord's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

MonkeRoom does not control information collected directly by Discord.

06Cookies & Website Technologies

The MonkeRoom website may use essential technologies required for normal website functionality.

If third-party services are added in the future, those services may use cookies, local storage, analytics, or similar technologies according to their own policies.

07Third-Party Services

MonkeRoom may rely on third-party services such as hosting providers, game servers, analytics providers, authentication services, Discord, or other infrastructure.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms. We recommend reviewing the policies of services you choose to use.

08Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

Certain technical, security, or moderation information may be retained longer when necessary to investigate abuse, maintain security, resolve disputes, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

09Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through MonkeRoom.

However, no website, game, server, or electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10Children's Privacy

MonkeRoom does not knowingly request or intentionally collect sensitive personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to MonkeRoom that should not have been collected, please contact the MonkeRoom team through the official community Discord.

11Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning your personal information.

  • Request information about personal data we process
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of certain information
  • Object to certain processing
  • Request restriction of processing
  • Request a copy of certain information

Some rights may be limited by applicable law or legitimate security and operational requirements.

12International Users

MonkeRoom may use servers or service providers located in countries different from your own.

As a result, information may be processed in jurisdictions outside your country of residence where permitted by applicable law.

13Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

When changes are made, the Last Updated date at the top of this page will be updated.

14Contact

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of information by MonkeRoom, please contact the MonkeRoom team through the official community Discord server.
