Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how MonkeRoom collects, uses, processes, and protects information through the game and its related services.

LAST UPDATED · NOVEMBER 17, 2026

01 Introduction

MonkeRoom is a multiplayer virtual reality game developed and operated by Primal Theory ("we", "us", or "our").

This Privacy Policy describes how information may be processed when you play MonkeRoom, use its online features, communicate with other players, or interact with our related services.

By using MonkeRoom, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with these practices, please discontinue using the game.

02 Information We Process

Depending on how you use MonkeRoom and which features are available, we and our service providers may process the following categories of information:

  • Account and platform information: platform-provided user identifiers, player identifiers, display names, and account-related information made available through supported platform services.
  • Gameplay information: usernames, player settings, inventory, virtual currency balances, purchased or unlocked content, statistics, progression, and multiplayer gameplay information.
  • Multiplayer information: Photon user identifiers, room identifiers, connection information, session data, and information required to synchronize multiplayer gameplay.
  • Voice communication: microphone audio transmitted through the game's multiplayer voice chat feature.
  • Purchase information: transaction references, purchase status, and entitlement information where applicable to purchases or digital content.
  • Technical information: diagnostic information, error reports, device and application information, and technical data processed by the platform or service providers.

The exact information processed depends on the features you use, your platform settings, and the services required to operate the game.

03 Meta Platform and Meta SDK

MonkeRoom uses Meta platform services and the Meta SDK to support functionality on compatible Meta Quest devices.

Depending on the platform functionality used, this may involve processing:

  • Platform-provided user identifiers.
  • Account or display name information made available by Meta.
  • Application entitlement and ownership verification information.
  • Information required for platform authentication and application functionality.

We use this information to identify players, support account-related functionality, verify access to the game, maintain security, and provide platform-compatible features.

Meta independently processes information in accordance with its own privacy policies and platform terms. MonkeRoom does not control all information collected directly by Meta.

More information is available in Meta's Privacy Policy.

04 Multiplayer Services — Photon

MonkeRoom uses Photon services, including Photon PUN, to provide multiplayer functionality.

These services may process information such as:

  • Photon user identifiers.
  • Room and session identifiers.
  • Connection and networking information.
  • Player state and gameplay information required for synchronization.

This information is used to connect players, maintain multiplayer sessions, synchronize gameplay, and help prevent abuse of online features.

Photon services are provided by Exit Games. Their processing of information is also subject to the applicable Photon privacy documentation and terms.

More information: Photon Engine.

05 Voice Chat — Photon Voice

MonkeRoom includes multiplayer voice communication functionality powered by Photon Voice.

When you use voice chat, microphone audio is transmitted through the voice communication service so that other participants in the relevant multiplayer session can hear you.

Voice communication is used to provide real-time communication between players. Your device and platform may require microphone permission for this feature.

MonkeRoom does not provide a feature intended to record players' voice conversations. However, the technical processing, transmission, and handling of voice data by the service provider are subject to Photon Voice's applicable terms and privacy practices.

You can control microphone access through your device's system settings and available in-game controls.

06 PlayFab and Game Backend

MonkeRoom uses Microsoft PlayFab services to support backend functionality and game-related account data.

Depending on the features you use, PlayFab may process information such as:

  • Player and account identifiers.
  • Player usernames and account-related settings.
  • Inventory, cosmetics, and unlocked digital items.
  • Virtual currency balances and related game transactions.
  • Game statistics and progression information.
  • Records needed to maintain account security and enforce game rules.

We use backend services to maintain player accounts, synchronize game data, manage inventory and virtual currency, deliver game functionality, and protect the game against fraud, cheating, and unauthorized access.
