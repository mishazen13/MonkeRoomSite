Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information MonkeRoom collects, how we use it, which services process it, and how you can request deletion of your data.

LAST UPDATED · NOVEMBER 19, 2026

01 Introduction

MonkeRoom is a multiplayer virtual reality game developed and operated by Primal Theory ("we", "us", or "our").

This Privacy Policy explains how Primal Theory collects, receives, uses, stores, and shares information when you play MonkeRoom, use its online features, or interact with our related services.

We collect certain information directly through the game and receive certain information from platform services, including Meta Platform Features, when you use the corresponding features.

02 Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, MonkeRoom collects information directly from your use of the game and receives information made available by connected platform and service providers.

Meta platform information:

  • Meta User ID (USER_ID): We collect and receive the platform-provided user identifier associated with your Meta account when using Meta authentication and related platform features. We use this identifier to recognize your player account, associate game data with the correct player, support account functionality, and help protect the game against cheating, abuse, and fraudulent activity.
  • Age group (USER_AGE): We receive and collect the age-group information made available by Meta through its platform features. This represents an age category or range rather than your full date of birth. We use it to support age-appropriate experiences, platform safety requirements, and applicable age-related restrictions.
  • In-app purchase information (IAP): When you make or use an eligible in-app purchase, we receive and collect purchase-related information made available by Meta. This may include product identifiers, purchase or transaction references, purchase status, entitlement information, and transaction timestamps where provided by the platform. We use this information to verify purchases, determine access to purchased content, deliver eligible items or rewards, and help prevent fraudulent or duplicate transactions.

Game and account information:

  • In-game username and player identifiers.
  • Player settings and preferences.
  • Inventory, cosmetics, and unlocked digital items.
  • Virtual currency balances and game-related transaction records.
  • Gameplay statistics, progression, and multiplayer activity.
  • Moderation, security, and ban-related records where necessary.

Multiplayer and technical information:

  • Photon user identifiers and room or session identifiers.
  • Connection and networking information.
  • Player state and gameplay information required for synchronization.
  • Technical diagnostics and error information processed by the game or its providers.

Voice communication information:

  • Microphone audio transmitted when you use the multiplayer voice chat feature.

We collect or receive only the information needed for the features and purposes described in this policy. The exact fields made available by third-party platforms may depend on the APIs and services used.

03 Meta Platform Features

MonkeRoom uses Meta SDK and Meta Platform Features on compatible Meta Quest devices. The following explains how the requested platform features are used.

USER_ID — User Identifier

We collect the Meta-provided user identifier when the game uses platform authentication or other functionality requiring player identification. This identifier helps us associate a player with their MonkeRoom account and related game records.

We use it for account identification, multiplayer account management, security checks, abuse prevention, investigation of cheating, and enforcement of game rules.

USER_AGE — User Age Group

We collect the age-group information provided by Meta when the relevant platform feature is used. This information is used to support age-related platform requirements, safety measures, and age-appropriate access to game features.

We do not need your full date of birth for this purpose. We use the age-group information made available by Meta rather than treating it as a complete identity record.

IAP — In-App Purchases

When you make an in-app purchase or when the game checks your purchase entitlements, we collect purchase-related information provided by Meta. This can include the purchased product identifier, transaction or purchase reference, purchase status, and entitlement information.

We use these details to confirm whether a purchase is valid, associate purchased content with the correct player, provide the purchased item or benefit, and prevent duplicate or fraudulent claims.

MonkeRoom does not need your full payment card information to perform these checks. Payment processing is handled by the applicable platform or payment provider.

Meta may independently collect and process additional information under its own privacy policies and platform terms. More information is available in Meta's Privacy Policy.

04 Multiplayer Services — Photon

MonkeRoom uses Photon PUN services to provide multiplayer functionality.

When you connect to multiplayer sessions, Photon may process user identifiers, room identifiers, session information, connection data, and gameplay state required to connect players and synchronize the game.

We use multiplayer information to operate online sessions, display and synchronize player activity, maintain connections, and help detect abuse of multiplayer features.

Photon services are provided by Exit Games. More information is available at Photon Engine.

05 Voice Chat — Photon Voice

MonkeRoom uses Photon Voice to provide real-time multiplayer voice communication.

When you enable or use voice chat, the game accesses microphone audio and transmits it through the voice communication service so other participants in the relevant session can hear you.

Voice audio is used to provide live communication between players. MonkeRoom does not provide a feature intended to record players' voice conversations. The technical processing and handling of voice data by the service provider are subject to Photon Voice's applicable terms and privacy practices.

You can manage microphone access through your device's system settings and available in-game controls.

06 PlayFab and Game Backend

MonkeRoom uses Microsoft PlayFab services to support backend functionality and game-related account data.

Depending on the features you use, we collect and store game information through PlayFab, including:

  • Player and account identifiers.
  • Player usernames and account-related settings.
  • Inventory, cosmetics, and unlocked digital items.
  • Virtual currency balances and related game records.
  • Game statistics and progression information.
  • Security, moderation, and account enforcement records where necessary.

We use this information to maintain player accounts, synchronize game data, manage inventory and virtual currency, provide game functionality, and protect the game against fraud, cheating, and unauthorized access.

PlayFab is provided by Microsoft. More information is available in the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

07 User IDs and Security

We use platform and game user identifiers to recognize players and associate relevant game records with the correct account.

These identifiers may be used to:

  • Detect and prevent cheating and exploitation.
  • Investigate suspicious activity and abuse reports.
  • Enforce bans and other moderation decisions.
  • Prevent fraudulent or unauthorized account activity.
  • Verify eligible purchases and related entitlements.
  • Deliver eligible digital content to the correct account.

We do not sell player identifiers or use them for unrelated advertising purposes.

08 Purchases and Digital Content

If you make an in-app purchase or acquire digital content in MonkeRoom, we may collect purchase-related information supplied by the applicable platform.

This information may include product identifiers, purchase references, transaction status, and entitlement information.

We use it to verify purchases, associate purchased content with your player account, provide eligible items or benefits, and prevent duplicate or fraudulent transactions.

Payment card details are handled by the applicable payment platform or provider, not collected by MonkeRoom for its own game account records.

09 Age and Platform Safety

MonkeRoom receives age-group information made available by Meta through the USER_AGE platform feature when applicable.

We use this information to support age-appropriate experiences, platform safety requirements, and applicable age-related restrictions.

We do not use age-group information for unrelated advertising purposes.

  • Verifying Meta platform access and entitlements.

15 Requesting Data Deletion

All MonkeRoom users can request deletion of their personal data free of charge, regardless of their country or geographic location.

You do not need to live in a particular country or rely on a specific regional privacy law to submit a deletion request.

To request deletion of your MonkeRoom data, send an email to:

Data Deletion Requests

Email our privacy contact directly:

michelzen13@gmail.com

Suggested email subject: MonkeRoom Data Deletion Request

Please include the following information in your request:

  1. Your MonkeRoom in-game username, if available.
  2. Your Meta User ID, if available.
  3. A short statement that you want your MonkeRoom personal data deleted.

Do not send your Meta password, payment card details, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

We may ask for reasonable information to verify that you own the account before deleting its data. This is intended to prevent unauthorized deletion requests.

After verifying a request, Primal Theory will delete or anonymize personal information associated with the account that is under our control, including applicable account identifiers, profile information, gameplay records, inventory information, and other associated personal data, where technically and legally possible.

Deletion of account-associated game data may result in loss of saved progress, inventory, virtual currency, unlocked content, and other account information. Deleted game progress may not be recoverable.

We may retain limited information where necessary to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, maintain transaction records, protect the security of the game, or prevent fraud and abuse. Where possible, retained information will be restricted to those purposes.

Information independently controlled by Meta, Microsoft, Photon, Discord, or another third-party provider may need to be deleted through that provider's own privacy or account-management procedures. We cannot directly delete information that is independently controlled by those providers.

We will review and handle valid deletion requests without undue delay and in accordance with applicable requirements.
