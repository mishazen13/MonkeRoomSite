Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how MonkeRoom collects, uses, processes, and protects information when you play our game or use our associated services.

Last Updated · November 13, 2026

01Who We Are

MonkeRoom is an independent multiplayer VR game developed and operated by Mikhail Korobkin.

For personal information processed by MonkeRoom under our control, Mikhail Korobkin is the data controller. This means that he determines why and how such information is processed.

This Privacy Policy applies to the MonkeRoom game and its related online services. Third-party services may have their own independent privacy policies.

02Information We Collect

We process information that is reasonably necessary to provide game functionality, multiplayer services, account management, security, and player support.

Depending on the features you use, this may include:

  • Meta account identifiers, including the Meta User ID or application-scoped user identifier made available through Meta services.
  • Meta account display information, such as an Oculus username, when made available and used by the game.
  • Entitlement or ownership verification results used to confirm access to MonkeRoom.
  • Player identifiers, usernames, account information, and game-related profile data.
  • In-game inventory, cosmetics, virtual currency, statistics, and progression information.
  • Photon network identifiers, multiplayer room information, connection status, and gameplay synchronization data.
  • Voice communication data when using Photon Voice multiplayer voice features.
  • Technical information such as IP addresses, connection information, device or application information, diagnostic information, and error reports processed by our service providers.
  • Information voluntarily provided when contacting our support team.

We do not intentionally collect more information than is reasonably necessary for the operation of the game.

03Meta Horizon Platform Data

MonkeRoom uses Meta platform services and the Meta SDK to provide functionality for Meta Quest devices.

Depending on the available permissions and platform functionality, we may receive and process information provided by Meta, including:

  • Meta User ID or an application-scoped account identifier.
  • Player display information made available by Meta.
  • Entitlement verification results.
  • Information required to authenticate a player and verify access to the application.

We use this information to identify players, verify ownership or entitlement to the game, provide account functionality, and maintain the security of MonkeRoom.

We do not intentionally use Meta account identifiers for unrelated advertising or sell them to third parties.

Meta may independently process information through its platform and services under its own policies. Our use of information received from Meta is subject to applicable Meta developer policies, including the Developer Data Use Policy.

Meta Privacy Policy: https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/

04PlayFab Services

MonkeRoom uses Microsoft PlayFab for backend game services and account-related functionality.

Depending on the features being used, PlayFab may process information such as:

  • PlayFab player identifiers and account information.
  • Player usernames and profile information.
  • Game statistics and progression.
  • Virtual currency and inventory information.
  • Game-related records used for security and moderation.
  • Identifiers used to associate a game account with a supported authentication provider, where configured.

We use PlayFab to manage player accounts, synchronize game data, maintain inventories, support multiplayer functionality, and protect the game against abuse.

If Meta account identifiers are linked to PlayFab accounts in a particular authentication flow, those identifiers may also be processed by PlayFab for that purpose.

PlayFab is a Microsoft service. Its processing of information is also governed by Microsoft's applicable service and privacy terms.

Microsoft Privacy Statement: Microsoft Privacy Statement

05Photon Multiplayer Services

MonkeRoom uses Photon PUN and Photon multiplayer infrastructure to provide online multiplayer gameplay.

Photon services may process information necessary to establish and maintain multiplayer sessions, including:

  • Photon user identifiers.
  • Room and session identifiers.
  • Connection and network information.
  • Player names and multiplayer-related data.
  • Gameplay synchronization and network events.

This information is used to connect players, synchronize gameplay, maintain multiplayer rooms, and support the operation and security of online sessions.

Photon Privacy Policy: https://www.photonengine.com/privacy

06Photon Voice and Audio Data

MonkeRoom may use Photon Voice to provide real-time voice communication between players.

When you use voice chat, audio captured through your device's microphone may be transmitted through the multiplayer voice service to provide communication with other players.

Voice communication is processed to provide the requested real-time functionality. We do not intentionally use voice recordings for advertising or unrelated purposes.

Microphone access is subject to the permissions and controls provided by your device and platform. You can avoid voice processing by not using voice communication features.

Audio transmission may be processed by Photon and its infrastructure providers according to their applicable terms and privacy policies.

07Purchases and In-Game Items

If in-game purchases are available, MonkeRoom may use Meta's purchase and entitlement services to verify transactions and provide purchased digital content.

Purchase-related information may include product identifiers, transaction or entitlement information, and purchase verification results.
