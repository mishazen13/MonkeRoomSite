Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This page explains what information MonkeRoom may process through the game, website and community, why it is used, and how you can contact us about your data.

LAST UPDATED · NOVEMBER 23, 2026

01Information We Collect

MonkeRoom is designed to collect information reasonably necessary to provide, maintain, secure and improve its services. The information processed depends on which features you use.

  • Meta platform user identifier used for account-related functionality.
  • Age group information provided through Meta Platform services, where applicable.
  • In-game username and player account identifiers.
  • In-app purchase information required to verify or provide purchased content.
  • IP address and connection information processed by networking and infrastructure providers.
  • Multiplayer session, room and gameplay information.
  • Game statistics and player progress.
  • Technical, diagnostic, moderation and anti-cheat information.
  • Discord user IDs, server IDs and configuration information when using related community features.

We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information that is not necessary for the operation of MonkeRoom.

02How We Use Information

Information may be processed for the following purposes:

  • Authenticate players and associate game data with their accounts.
  • Provide multiplayer functionality and synchronize gameplay.
  • Process or verify in-app purchases.
  • Save player progress and game-related data.
  • Prevent cheating, abuse, fraud and malicious activity.
  • Moderate the game and community.
  • Diagnose errors and improve stability.
  • Maintain the security and operation of MonkeRoom.

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

03Meta Platform Services

MonkeRoom uses Meta Platform services to support account authentication and platform-related functionality.

Depending on the features used and permissions granted, MonkeRoom may receive or process your Meta user ID, age group information and in-app purchase information.

This information is used for account identification, eligibility-related functionality and purchase verification. Meta processes information under its own privacy policy and platform terms.

04PlayFab Services

MonkeRoom uses Microsoft PlayFab as part of its backend infrastructure.

PlayFab may process player identifiers, usernames, virtual currency balances, inventory, statistics and other game-related information needed to provide account and gameplay features.

This information is used to save player data, provide game functionality, manage inventory and support security and moderation systems.

05Photon Multiplayer and Voice

MonkeRoom uses Photon services for multiplayer networking and, where enabled, voice communication.

Photon may process connection information, player identifiers, room information, network data and voice communication data required to provide these features.

Multiplayer and voice functionality may require data to be transmitted between players and Photon infrastructure. Photon handles information under its own privacy policy.

06Discord

MonkeRoom provides an official Discord community. If you join or interact with the community, Discord processes your information under its own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Community bots or related services may process Discord user IDs, server IDs, command usage, configuration and moderation information.

07Cookies and Website Technologies

The MonkeRoom website may use local storage or similar browser technologies to remember preferences such as your selected color theme.

The website may load third-party resources, including fonts and hosting infrastructure. Those providers may process technical connection information when your browser requests their resources.

08Third-Party Services

MonkeRoom relies on third-party providers to operate certain features. These may include:

  • Meta Platform services.
  • Microsoft PlayFab.
  • Photon networking and voice services.
  • Discord.
  • Website hosting and infrastructure providers.

These providers process information according to their own privacy policies, terms and applicable agreements. MonkeRoom does not control their independent data practices.

09Data Retention

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant game, account, multiplayer, security or community functionality.

Certain information may be retained for longer when necessary to prevent abuse, investigate security incidents, resolve disputes or comply with legal obligations.

10Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through MonkeRoom.

However, no online service, network transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11Children's Privacy

MonkeRoom does not knowingly request or intentionally collect personal information from children beyond what is necessary for permitted platform and game functionality.

If you believe that information has been collected improperly, please contact us using the details provided below.

12Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including:

  • Request access to personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of personal information.
  • Object to certain processing activities.
  • Request restriction of processing.
  • Request a copy of certain information.

Some rights may be limited by applicable law or legitimate security and operational requirements.

13Data Deletion Requests

You can request deletion of information associated with your MonkeRoom account by contacting the development team.

Send an email using the subject MonkeRoom Data Deletion Request and include your Meta user ID or another account identifier that allows us to locate your data.

We may need to verify that you control the account before processing a deletion request. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or necessary for legitimate security purposes.

Information may therefore be processed in jurisdictions outside your country of residence, subject to applicable data protection requirements.
